🫙 Preservation — Drying · Freezing · Tincture
🌿
Drying
Bundle and hang in shade 1–2 weeks. Strip leaves and store in sealed jar. Best used within 6 months.
❄️
Freezing
Blend fresh basil with olive oil and freeze in ice cube trays. Goes directly into pasta, soups, and sauces all winter.

Pack fresh basil in 80-proof vodka, steep 4 weeks. 1–2 droppers in water daily as an adaptogenic tonic.

Basil Salt
Layer fresh basil leaves with coarse sea salt — 1 part basil to 4 parts salt. Seal and leave 2 weeks. The salt draws out the oils and becomes an herb-infused finishing salt. Use on eggs, pasta, roasted chicken, and grilled fish. Gift it. Sell it.
Basil Lemonade
Make a basil simple syrup — simmer equal parts sugar and water with a big handful of fresh basil 15 minutes off heat. Strain. Add to fresh-squeezed lemonade over ice with a fresh basil leaf on top. Farmers market gold.
Basil Tea
8–10 fresh leaves steeped in hot water 5–7 minutes. Add honey and lemon. Drink in the evening for gentle stress relief and digestive support.

🫙 Preservation — Drying · Freezing · Tincture

❄️
Freezing
Peel and freeze whole rhizomes. Grate directly from frozen. Best method for preserving fresh turmeric for cooking.
🫙
Tincture
Pack fresh sliced turmeric in 80-proof vodka or coconut oil (fat-soluble extraction). Steep 4–6 weeks. 1–2 droppers daily in water or milk for anti-inflammatory support. Always take with black pepper to enhance absorption.

Golden Milk
Simmer 1 cup milk or oat milk with 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1/4 teaspoon black pepper (essential!), 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon, and a pinch of ginger for 5 minutes. Add honey. Drink before bed. The black pepper increases curcumin absorption by up to 2000% — it is not optional. Consistent daily use for 6 weeks produces measurable anti-inflammatory results.
Fresh Turmeric Pickle
Peel and slice fresh turmeric rhizomes thin. Combine with julienned ginger, garlic slivers, and salt. Pack into a jar with lemon juice and a drizzle of olive oil. Refrigerate 24 hours before eating. This pickle keeps 2 weeks refrigerated and is a powerful anti-inflammatory condiment.
