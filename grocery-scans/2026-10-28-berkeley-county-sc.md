## 📍 Bottom Line
- **For meat, go to Aldi and Food Lion.** Aldi has the cheapest ground beef and tenderloins. Food Lion has the cheapest whole chicken and pork chops.
- **If you're near a Lidl,** the $0.99/lb leg quarters are the best price on chicken this week. You need the Lidl Plus app.
- **Publix is the BOGO store this week.** Get the berries, Just Bare chicken, marinated pork, tea and lemonade, and dairy there.
- **Buy soda at Food Lion.** Pepsi is 3 for $10 and Coke 12-packs are buy one get one free.
- **A simple plan:** Aldi for beef and chicken, Food Lion for whole birds, pork and soda, Publix for BOGOs and berries. Skip Harris Teeter unless you check its ad first.
