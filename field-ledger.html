Questions

Answers first.

Do I need to know how to use Google Sheets?

Not really. If you can type numbers into a box, you can use the Field Ledger. Everything is already labeled and formatted. There's no formula-writing, no setup from scratch — you just make a copy and start entering your numbers.

Will this file do my taxes for me?

No — and I want to be clear about that upfront. I'm not your tax preparer. The Field Ledger organizes your income and expenses so that when you sit down with your preparer (or use tax software), all your numbers are already in one place. The heavy lifting is done. The filing is still up to you.

I'm a farmer or homesteader — is this right for me?

Yes. The Field Ledger includes categories for livestock, feed, seeds, supplies, and farmers market fees alongside the standard Schedule C categories. If you sell eggs, plants, mushrooms, seedlings, or animals — those are self-employment income and you need to track them the same way any other business does.

What if I have more than one income stream?

The ledger is designed with multiple income streams in mind. You can log different income sources and label them — notary work, field inspections, farmers market, Etsy sales, gig work — and see them all in one running total.

Is there a subscription or renewal fee?

No. You get access once, make a copy to your own Google Drive, and it's yours permanently. No monthly charge, no annual renewal, no expiration date.

Can I share it with my tax preparer?

Yes. You can share the Google Sheet directly with your preparer as view-only access, or download it as an Excel file to send. Either way works.

What if I can't afford it right now?

Scroll up to the contribution section. If the tool helped you, leave what you can whenever you can. Getting organized shouldn't have a price barrier during a lean season.
