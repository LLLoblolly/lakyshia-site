Green Tea
Camellia sinensis · Leaf
The world's most consumed antioxidant-rich beverage. Contains EGCG — a powerful polyphenol linked to longevity, fat metabolism, and brain health. Provides calm, sustained energy through its unique combination of caffeine and L-theanine.
Yerba Maté
Ilex paraguariensis · Leaf
The sacred drink of South America, beloved for its clean, sustained energy — the strength of coffee, the health of tea, the euphoria of chocolate. Rich in 24 vitamins and minerals, 15 amino acids, and powerful antioxidants. Nourishes body and mind.
Moringa
Moringa oleifera · Leaf
Called the "miracle tree," moringa contains more vitamin C than oranges, more calcium than milk, more iron than spinach, and more potassium than bananas — all in one leaf. Used in traditional medicine across Africa, India, and the Caribbean for centuries.
Nettle
Urtica dioica · Leaf
One of the most nutrient-dense plants on earth. Nettle is rich in iron, calcium, magnesium, and vitamins A, C, and K. A traditional spring tonic used to cleanse the blood, reduce inflammation, support kidneys, and restore energy after winter.
